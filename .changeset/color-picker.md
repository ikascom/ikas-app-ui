Add `@ikas/color-picker`: an outline field that grows into a panel (SPRING, sections entering on `panel-enter`) with a saturation and brightness area, hue and opacity sliders, a text field that reads and cycles HEX, RGB, HSL and OKLCH, an eyedropper where the browser has one, an optional WCAG contrast readout (`contrastWith`) and saved colors you can add, reorder (drag or Alt+arrows) and delete. Emits hex. No new npm dependency.
